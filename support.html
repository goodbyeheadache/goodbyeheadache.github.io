GoodbyeHeadache Support

GoodbyeHeadache is a concussion-recovery training app with adaptive vision, balance and cognitive exercises. It supports recovery and is not a substitute for medical care. Stop any exercise if your symptoms worsen, and seek medical care immediately for red-flag symptoms.

Contact

Questions, feedback, or problems with the app: info@goodbyeheadache.com. We aim to reply within two business days.

Common questions

How do I delete my account? In the app, open the You tab and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all recovery data. You can also email us to request deletion.

How do I manage my subscription? Subscriptions are billed by the App Store or Google Play. Manage or cancel them in your device's subscription settings.

I signed in with Apple and my name is missing. Apple shares your name only on the first sign-in. To fix it, remove GoodbyeHeadache under Settings → Apple ID → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple, then sign in again and choose to share your name.

Daily reminder not arriving? Check that notifications are allowed for GoodbyeHeadache in your device settings, then re-save the reminder time in the You tab.
