Privacy Policy

GoodbyeHeadache · Effective 25 September 2026

GoodbyeHeadache ("the app") is a concussion-recovery training app operated by Goodbyeheadache Inc., Alberta, Canada ("we", "us"). Your recovery data is sensitive, and this policy explains exactly what we collect, why, where it lives, and how you delete it. The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, and we never use it for advertising.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, browsing activity, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Who processes it

The app is built on Google Firebase (Google LLC): Firebase Authentication signs you in and Cloud Firestore stores your account's data, encrypted in transit and at rest. Firebase processes this data on our behalf under Google's data-processing terms. If you sign in with Google or Apple, those providers handle the sign-in itself under their own privacy policies; we receive only your name, email, and a sign-in token.

What stays on your device

A local copy of your training data is cached on your device so the app works offline. On Android we explicitly exclude this data from Google's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your health data never leaves the device through backups. Daily training reminders are scheduled locally on your device — no reminder data is sent anywhere.

Access

Your recovery data is readable and writable only by your own signed-in account, enforced by server-side security rules. We do not share it with anyone, sell it, or use it for advertising or profiling. We would disclose data only if legally required to.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app (You → Delete account). This permanently removes your sign-in account and all recovery data stored under it, and wipes the local cache on the device. If you signed in with Apple, the app also revokes its Sign in with Apple authorization. You can also email us (below) to request deletion.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or similar laws), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or erase your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Contact us and we will honour these requests.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Not medical advice

GoodbyeHeadache supports recovery training but is not a medical device and does not replace professional medical care. The app repeats this disclaimer, along with red-flag warning signs that need urgent medical attention, inside the product itself.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date above and notify you in the app before the change applies to you.
