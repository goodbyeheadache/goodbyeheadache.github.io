Privacy Policy

GoodbyeHeadache · Effective 28 September 2026

GoodbyeHeadache ("the app") is a concussion-recovery training app operated by Goodbyeheadache Inc., Alberta, Canada ("we", "us"). Your recovery data is sensitive, and this policy explains exactly what we collect, why, where it lives, how long we keep it, and how you delete it. The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, and we never use it for advertising.

GoodbyeHeadache is a direct-to-consumer wellness app. It is not a clinical service, and we are not a health-care provider or a HIPAA-covered entity. We still treat your recovery information as sensitive and handle it in line with Canada's PIPEDA, Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act, and other privacy laws that apply where you live.

Your health information and your consent

Your symptom check-ins, exercise results, and cardio baseline describe your health, so we treat them as sensitive information. By creating an account and entering this information, you consent to us processing it for the purposes in this policy. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account, which removes the data (see "Deleting your data"). We will never sell health information, use it for advertising, or disclose it to third parties without your explicit consent, except to the service providers named below or where the law requires.

How we use it

We use your information to run the app and personalise your program; adapt exercise difficulty to your symptoms and accuracy; show your progress and outcome trends; generate the progress summary you can print for your practitioner; send the account emails described below; keep your account secure and prevent abuse; and meet our legal obligations. For people in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and similar jurisdictions, our legal bases are: performing our contract with you (running the app), your explicit consent (health information), our legitimate interests (security, preventing fraud, keeping the app working), and compliance with law.

Adaptive difficulty and automated decisions

The app automatically raises or lowers exercise levels based on your accuracy and symptom reports. This is automated processing, but it only changes the difficulty of your exercises; it has no legal or similarly significant effect on you, and you stay in control: every exercise has self-adjust controls, and easing a level down is never blocked. We do not use your data to profile you for any other purpose.

Who processes it

The app is built on Google Firebase (Google LLC): Firebase Authentication signs you in and Cloud Firestore stores your account's data, encrypted in transit and at rest. Firebase processes this data on our behalf under Google's data-processing terms. If you sign in with Google or Apple, those providers handle the sign-in itself under their own privacy policies; we receive only your name, email, and a sign-in token. Sign in with Apple also lets you hide your email address behind Apple's private relay. Subscriptions are processed by the App Store or Google Play under their own privacy terms; we receive your subscription status but never your payment-card details.

What stays on your device

A local copy of your training data is cached on your device so the app works offline. On Android we explicitly exclude this data from Google's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your health data never leaves the device through backups. Daily training reminders are scheduled locally on your device — no reminder data is sent anywhere.

Access and sharing

Your recovery data is readable and writable only by your own signed-in account, enforced by server-side security rules. We do not share it with anyone, sell it, "share" it for cross-context advertising, or use it for profiling. We would disclose data only if legally required to (for example, a valid court order), to protect someone's safety, or, if our business is ever sold or merged, to the successor under this same policy — and we would tell you first.

We may create aggregated or de-identified statistics — for example, how often an exercise level is completed — to improve the exercises and understand how the app is used. This data cannot reasonably be used to identify you, and we commit not to try to re-identify it.

Where your data is processed

Goodbyeheadache Inc. is based in Alberta, Canada. Firebase may store and process your data on servers in the United States or other countries, where privacy laws may differ from those in your home country. Where required, transfers are protected by recognised safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards that apply.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your account record and recovery data are removed from our live systems immediately and from backups within 30 days. Technical logs are kept for up to 90 days. We may keep de-identified statistics indefinitely, and we may retain limited records longer where the law requires it (for example, subscription transaction records).

Communications

We send only the emails needed to run your account: verification, password reset, security notices, and legally required notices. We do not send marketing emails unless you ask for them, and any such email would include an unsubscribe link, consistent with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation. Daily training reminders are notifications generated on your device; you can turn them off in the You tab or in your device's settings.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards: encryption in transit and at rest, per-account access rules enforced on the server, least-privilege access for our team, and verified email addresses. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will notify you and the relevant privacy authorities as required by PIPEDA, Alberta's PIPA, and other applicable laws.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app (You → Delete account). This permanently removes your sign-in account and all recovery data stored under it, and wipes the local cache on the device. If you signed in with Apple, the app also revokes its Sign in with Apple authorization. You can also email us to request deletion. Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription — cancel that in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Your rights

Everyone. You can access, correct, export, or erase your personal data, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days, after verifying your identity. We will never discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Canada. Under PIPEDA and Alberta's PIPA you may request access to your personal information and challenge its accuracy. If you are unhappy with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta. Residents of Quebec have equivalent rights under Law 25, and our person in charge of the protection of personal information can be reached at the contact address below.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland. You have the rights above under the GDPR, plus the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

United States. Residents of California and other states with privacy laws have the right to know, access, correct, and delete personal information, and to opt out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising. We do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you are under the age of majority where you live, use the app only with the consent of a parent or guardian and with your clinician's knowledge. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party links and services

The app and our website may link to third-party sites or services (for example, the App Store, Google Play, or your device's settings). Their privacy practices are their own; this policy does not cover them.

Not medical advice

GoodbyeHeadache supports recovery training but is not a medical device and does not replace professional medical care. The app repeats this disclaimer, along with red-flag warning signs that need urgent medical attention, inside the product itself.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date above and notify you in the app before the change applies to you. Continued use after that date means you accept the updated policy.
